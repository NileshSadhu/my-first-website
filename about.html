Company Background

Specialized Project Management Consultancy (PMC)

The construction industry generally deals with various sectors viz. Real Estate & Infrastructure. The Real Estate Sector is segmented into Residential and Commercial developments. To manage such unique projects requires expertise with organizations and a thorough body of knowledge.

Project Management Consultancy plays a multifaceted role in such developments, providing comprehensive services from inception to completion. At every stage of the project life cycle, the principles of pro-activeness and creating win-win situations are vital, keeping the customer and client requirements at the forefront.

“Use of Project Management Consultancy (PMC) offers one of the most effective management solutions to increase and improve the efficiency and outcome of a project in construction.”

We undertake a planned series of activities designed in synchronization with our customers' vision whenever we embark on new projects. This includes overall planning, coordination, monitoring, and controlling from inception to closeout to produce functionally and financially viable developments.

Our Methodology

Structured Approach to Civil Execution

Project Management Consultants manage projects by the application of knowledge, skills, and experience at various stages. However, PMC also has to navigate challenges like permissions from BMC & SRA Authority, constructability issues, and multi-contractor synchronization.

Statutory & Authority Approvals

Direct liaison and proactive clearance handling with BMC (Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation) and SRA (Slum Rehabilitation Authority) to ensure uninterrupted project progression.

Executive Management & Quality Audits

Adopting high-level tools like Round Table Progress Reviews, quality diligence, safety compliance audits, and regular delivery sessions.

Total Project Lifecycle Management

PMC is most effective when involved in the Total Project Lifecycle from Conception to Closeout, rigorously controlling Time, Cost, Risk, Scope, Quality, and Resources.
