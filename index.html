About M B Realty

Specialized Project Management Consultancy & Construction

The construction industry generally deals with various sectors viz. Real Estate & Infrastructure, segmented in Residential and Commercial developments. To manage such unique projects requires expertise with organizations and a thorough body of knowledge.

Project Management Consultancy (PMC) plays a multifaceted role, offering services from inception to completion. At every stage of the project life cycle, principles of pro-activeness and creating win-win situations are paramount.

“In order to deliver end-to-end project delivery on time, within authorized cost & match the expected quality standards.”

Under our PMC services, we undertake planned activities designed in synchronization with client requirements. This includes overall planning, coordination, monitoring, and controlling from inception to closeout, handling statutory BMC and SRA Authority permissions with well-organized approaches.
