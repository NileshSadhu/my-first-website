Our Methodology

The 5-Stage PMC Execution Lifecycle

A systematic, transparent roadmap designed to navigate complex engineering constraints and statutory requirements from project inception to final key handover.

01

Inception & Feasibility

Plot boundary survey, FSI/TDR calculation, financial modeling, structural feasibility audit, and comprehensive DPR drafting.

02

Sanctions & Sanctions

Statutory BMC / SRA filing, LOI & IOA sanctions, CFO Fire NOC, Environmental Clearance, and tenant consent registry.

03

Tendering & Contracts

Preparation of detailed BOQ, transparent competitive bidding, contractor financial vetting, and stringent DA legal covenants.

04

Site PMC Execution

Daily structural supervision, material lab testing, CPM milestone tracking, safety enforcement, and billing reconciliation.

05

OC & Handover

Fire final NOC, BMC building completion certification, Occupation Certificate (OC) procurement, and snag-free member possession.
